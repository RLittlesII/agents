name: spec
description: "Take a raw ask to a decided need, then to the agreement sections and the scenario file of a Feature specification."
---

Follow the artifact ownership and review boundaries in the repository guidance.

Use these skills: `clarify-requirements` (`.agents/skills/clarify-requirements/SKILL.md`), `spec-and-traceability` (`.agents/skills/spec-and-traceability/SKILL.md`).

The agreement owner explores the ask in discovery mode: who wants it,
what failure state it removes, settled answers and material unknowns. Once the
asker confirms the recorded need, the author writes the agreement and scenarios.
The reviewer returns independent findings and an agreement verdict. The author
addresses agreement findings and records the returned verdict in sign-off.
The output is the specification, scenarios and recorded review evidence.
